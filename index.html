    Malaviya Mission Teacher Training Centre, DAVV in Association with School of Data Science and Forecasting Conducts Short Term Course in Cyber Security and Internet Ethics

    Organized by Malaviya Mission Teacher Training Centre (Formerly UGC-HRDC), DAVV Indore in association with School of Data Science and Forecasting (SDSF) • Coordinator: Dr. Govind Maheshwari

    Organized by MMTTC (UGC-HRDC), DAVV in association with School of Data Science & Forecasting (SDSF)
    Coordinator: Dr. Govind Maheshwari • Online Mode • 26 Oct – 31 Oct 2026

    The Malaviya Mission Teacher Training Centre (formally UGC-HRDC), Devi Ahilya Vishwavidyalaya, Indore (M.P.), in association with the School of Data Science & Forecasting (SDSF), successfully conducted a dedicated Short Term Course on Cyber Security and Internet Ethics from 26th to 31st October 2026 in Online Mode. The course was coordinated by Dr. Govind Maheshwari (Head, School of Data Science & Forecasting, DAVV). The comprehensive program empowered educators and faculty members with practical skills to navigate today's dynamic digital threat landscape and maintain rigorous ethical standards in academia.

    Key Course Objectives

    • To equip faculty with practical cybersecurity and digital ethics skills to protect personal and institutional data.
    • To enable educators to integrate internet ethics, safe browsing, and data privacy principles seamlessly into their everyday academic teaching.
    In Association With: School of Data Science & Forecasting (SDSF)
    Course Coordinator: Dr. Govind Maheshwari
    Course Fee: No Registration Fee (Limited Seats)
    Course Mode: Online Mode
    Daily Schedule: 10:00 AM – 1:00 PM & 2:00 PM – 5:00 PM
    Duration: 26-Oct-2026 to 31-Oct-2026

    Essential Requirements for Online Participants

    • NOC / Permission from Participant's Institute
    • Desktop / Laptop with reliable high-speed Internet connectivity
    • Webcam, Headphone / Microphone

    🏆 SDSF's Yash Mathur Clinches Runners-Up at District Men's Football Championship

    Representing DAVV UTD with pride, Yash Mathur brings home silver from the District Level Men's Football Competition held at Govt. College Rau, Indore.

    Blog By Om Singh Lodhi (M.Tech AI&DS)
    30 October 2026 • Govt. College Rau, Indore

    The School of Data Science and Forecasting (SDSF), DAVV, is proud to celebrate the achievement of its student Yash Mathur, who represented the DAVV UTD Football Team at the prestigious District Level Men's Football Competition held at Government College, Rau, Indore, from 28th to 30th October 2026.

    Competing against teams from across the district, Yash and the DAVV UTD squad demonstrated exceptional skill, teamwork, and competitive spirit throughout the tournament. The team's stellar run in the competition culminated in a well-deserved Runners-Up (Second Position) finish — a remarkable feat that reflects both individual talent and collective effort on the field.

    Achievement Highlights

    • Tournament: District Level Men's Football Competition
    • Venue: Government College, Rau, Indore
    • Duration: 28th – 30th October 2026
    • Result: 🥈 Runners-Up (Second Position)
    • Team: DAVV UTD Football Team

    This achievement is a testament to Yash's dedication and passion for the sport, and SDSF proudly extends its heartfelt congratulations to him. We look forward to seeing him continue to represent DAVV and SDSF on bigger stages in the future. 🎉⚽

CAMPUS & CULTURE • FESTIVE SPECIAL 14/09/2026

SDSF Welcomes “SDSF Ke Raja” with Grandeur and Devotion

A vibrant celebration of faith, togetherness, and student spirit at the School of Data Science & Forecasting

Blog By Joyobrata Das (M.Tech AI&DS) Student Press
14 September 2026 • SDSF, DAVV, Indore

The School of Data Science & Forecasting (SDSF), DAVV, Indore, came alive with devotion, enthusiasm, and festive spirit on 14th September 2026 as students and members of the SDSF family gathered to celebrate the auspicious Agaman (arrival) of Lord Ganesha, lovingly welcomed as “SDSF Ke Raja.”

The celebration marked the beginning of a spiritually uplifting and joyful period for the SDSF community. With beautifully decorated surroundings, vibrant traditional arrangements, and the chants of “Ganapati Bappa Morya!”, the campus radiated an atmosphere of positivity and togetherness.

A Grand Agaman of Bappa

The arrival of SDSF Ke Raja was celebrated with great excitement as students came together to welcome Bappa with traditional rituals and heartfelt devotion. The campus echoed with chants and devotional music, creating a memorable beginning to the festivities.

Students from different semesters actively participated in the arrangements, contributing their time, creativity, and energy to make the celebration special. From decorations and event coordination to devotional activities, the entire celebration reflected the collaborative spirit of SDSF.

Bhajan Sandhya — Music, Devotion & Togetherness

One of the highlights of the celebration was the Bhajan Sandhya, where students from different semesters came together to offer their musical tributes to Lord Ganesha.

The evening witnessed soulful bhajans, devotional songs, and collective chanting, with students taking turns to showcase their musical talents. The atmosphere became especially vibrant as students joined in the singing, transforming the gathering into a beautiful evening of music, faith, and fellowship.

The Bhajan Sandhya also provided a wonderful opportunity for students across different batches and semesters to interact, participate, and celebrate together beyond the classroom.

Students Take the Lead

The festivities were not limited to prayers and bhajans. Students enthusiastically hosted and participated in several activities, taking responsibility for different aspects of the celebration.

From creative decorations, rangoli, event arrangements, anchoring, photography, music, and coordination to managing the overall festive setup, students played an active role throughout the celebration.

Their enthusiasm and teamwork demonstrated the strong sense of community that exists within SDSF. Every contribution, whether big or small, helped turn the occasion into a memorable celebration.

A Celebration Beyond the Classroom

The celebration of SDSF Ke Raja was more than a religious observance—it became a celebration of unity, creativity, culture, and belonging.

Students and faculty came together to share moments of joy and devotion, strengthening the bond within the SDSF community. The festivities provided a refreshing break from academic routines while giving students an opportunity to showcase their talents, take initiative, and work together as a team.

As the celebrations concluded for the day, the spirit of “Ganapati Bappa Morya” remained alive across the SDSF campus, leaving behind memories of smiles, music, devotion, and togetherness.

SDSF Welcomes Bappa

With hearts filled with devotion and the campus filled with festive energy, SDSF proudly welcomed “SDSF Ke Raja” on 14th September 2026.

The celebration beautifully captured the essence of Ganesh Chaturthi—faith in the heart, joy in togetherness, and positivity in every beginning.

Ganapati Bappa Morya! Mangal Murti Morya! 🌺🙏

Aagaaz 26: SDSF Welcomes Its New Batch with Colour, Culture and Conviction

View Gallery
SDSF, DAVV Takshila Campus
Indore, M.P. • 2026 Batch Induction
Saraswati Vandana Lamp Lighting VC Address Cultural Night Felicitations Industry Guests

The School of Data Science and Forecasting (SDSF), DAVV Takshila Campus, rolled out a vibrant welcome for its 2026 batch as it hosted its annual induction ceremony, Aagaaz 26. The event opened on a note of devotion and pride with the Saraswati Vandana and the Kulgeet, followed by the ceremonial lighting of the lamp by the dignitaries on stage.

The occasion was graced by Department Coordinator Dr. Shishir Kumar Shandhilya, Department HOD Dr. Govind Maheswari, the Honourable Vice Chancellor (Kulpati) Dr. Rakesh Singhai as Chief Guest, along with special guests Mr. Rajat Bajaj (Rakuten) and Mr. Bhupendra, CEO of Bitbyte Software Technology.

The faculty addressed the incoming students with words of encouragement, while Dr. Rakesh Singhai's address stood out as the highlight of the morning — speaking on the importance of alumni and professional connections, giving an illustrative example of SDSF's own alumni journey. He made one special mention: the SDSF Official Website, which came out of the department itself, is already reaching students and professionals beyond the campus.

"You are not just students — you are the future architects of data-driven India. Make every day of this journey count."
Dr. Rakesh Singhai, Vice Chancellor, DAVV

The ceremony then moved from formal recognition to festive celebration, as faculty members and students gathered for a group photograph marking the occasion. What followed was an evening full of youthful energy — first-year students, alongside seniors and final-year batchmates, took the stage for dance and musical performances brimming with enthusiasm. A lively "guess the logo" game engaged the freshers, testing their wit amid laughter and applause, while an introduction video gave the newcomers a warm, visual glimpse into the spirit and legacy of SDSF. Aagaaz 26 closed not just as an induction, but as a fitting reminder of the department's blend of academic ambition and genuine camaraderie.

Aagaaz 26 was more than just an orientation — it was a declaration of SDSF's culture: rooted in rigour, alive with spirit, and ready for the future. The 2026 batch is officially home. 🎓

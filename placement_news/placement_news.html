    Monthly Website Updates

    Students are encouraged to submit updates about their achievements and projects to the SDSF tech team for consideration and possible publication on the department's official website.

    The School of Data Science and Forecasting (SDSF) invites all students to share updates about their personal academic accomplishments, independent software or AI projects, research publications, or competitions.

    Submission Guidelines

    • Send your submission by email to the tech team at the addresses below.
    • Include student name, program, semester, and a concise summary with relevant links/photos.
    • Selected noteworthy submissions will be featured on the official website & Chronicle.

    Sept 2, 2026 • Orange Data Tech

    Six SDSF Students Selected at Orange Data Tech Through Campus Hiring Drive

    The School of Data Science & Forecasting (SDSF), Devi Ahilya Vishwavidyalaya (DAVV), Indore, is pleased to announce that six students from the department have successfully secured placements at Orange Data Tech through the campus hiring drive.

    The recruitment drive provided students with an excellent opportunity to demonstrate their analytical, technical, problem-solving, and communication skills. The selection process included multiple stages designed to assess the candidates' aptitude, technical knowledge, and suitability for the respective roles.

    Roles Offered:
    Business Analyst (BA) Associate Software Engineer

    This achievement reflects the dedication, hard work, and professional preparedness of our students, along with the continuous efforts of the faculty and the Placement Cell in creating meaningful career opportunities for SDSF students.

    The School of Data Science & Forecasting extends its heartiest congratulations to all six selected students and wishes them great success in their upcoming professional journey.

    We also express our sincere gratitude to Orange Data Tech for providing this valuable campus recruitment opportunity to our students.

    Congratulations to our achievers!

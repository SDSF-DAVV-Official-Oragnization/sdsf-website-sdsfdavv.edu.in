    Monthly Website Updates

    Students are encouraged to submit updates about their achievements and projects to the SDSF tech team for consideration and possible publication on the department's official website.

    The School of Data Science and Forecasting (SDSF) invites all students to share updates about their personal academic accomplishments, independent software or AI projects, research publications, or competitions.

    Submission Guidelines

    • Send your submission by email to the tech team at the addresses below.
    • Include student name, program, semester, and a concise summary with relevant links/photos.
    • Selected noteworthy submissions will be featured on the official website & Chronicle.

    Detailed Admission Information

    S. No. Name of Programme Eligibility
    01. MBA (Business Analytics)
    CUET PG 2 Yrs    		 Higher Secondary (10+2) with Mathematics as a subject + Any Bachelor degree with at least 50% marks in aggregate or an equivalent grade for General/OBC candidates, and 45% marks in aggregate or an equivalent grade for SC/ST and Differently Abled (DA) category candidates from a recognized University / Institute.
    02. M. Tech (Dual Degree)
    Artificial Intelligence & Data Science
    CUET UG 5 Yrs    		 Higher Secondary (10+2) with Science stream and Mathematics as a subject with at least 50% marks in aggregate or an equivalent grade for General/OBC candidates, and 45% marks in aggregate or an equivalent grade for SC/ST and Differently Abled (DA) category candidates from a recognized Board.
    03. M.Sc. (Data Science & Analytics)
    Non CUET PG 2 Yrs    		 Bachelor degree with Mathematics / Statistics / Computer Science as one of the main subject OR B.E./ B.Tech. in any discipline with at least 50% marks in aggregate or an equivalent grade for General / OBC candidates, and 45% marks in aggregate or an equivalent grade for SC/ST and Differently Abled (DA) category candidates from a recognized University/Institute.
    04. M. Tech. (Data Science)
    Non CUET PG 2 Yrs
    (AICTE Approved)    		 At least 55% aggregate marks in B.E./B.Tech. in any relevant branch of Engineering or Masters degree in Physics / Mathematics/ Statistics / Computer Science or any other equivalent degree. Relaxation of 5% marks in eligibility for sponsored/SC/ST candidates.
    05. M. Tech. (Big Data Analytics)
    Non CUET PG 2 Yrs
    (AICTE Approved)    		 At least 55% aggregate marks in B.E./B.Tech. in any relevant branch of Engineering or Masters degree in Physics / Mathematics/ Statistics / Computer Science or any other equivalent degree. Relaxation of 5% marks in eligibility for sponsored/SC/ST candidates.
    06. M. Tech. Executive
    (Data Science)
    Non CUET PG 2 Yrs    		 The candidate should have at least 55% aggregate marks in B.E. / B.Tech./ M.Sc. in a relevant subject or any other equivalent degree (including M.C.A.) from a recognised University. Relaxation of 5% marks in eligibility for SC/ ST candidates. Relevant subjects for admission in M.Tech. Data Science, are CS/CSE/IT/Maths/Physics/ Statistics/ Computer Applications or other computer related subjects.
